# Concepts and glossary

Lighthouse provides a small, readable interface between a captain and a coding fleet. You supply goals and
limits; firstmate manages the coding work. An operator watches the fleet, explains what changed, keeps the board
current and brings back decisions that require you. The board is a record and communication surface, not a
second task scheduler inside firstmate.

## Roles

- **Captain:** you, the human deciding what to do. A configurable display name does not change the internal `owner` identity.
- **Firstmate:** the fleet coordinator. Its own task records remain its record of work.
- **Operator:** the supervising session beside firstmate. It translates, checks instruments and relays approved words.
- **Watch operator:** a separate operator that reads conduct reports and issues notices about the operating contract.
- **Advice steward:** an independent decision assistant. It proposes, compares and explains; it never acts for you.
- **Chief developer:** a development role, not a source of authority to change your goals.

## Four disciplines

Observe before interpreting. Keep the board accurate. Use plain language that separates facts from judgments. Bring
a captain decision back as a clear question rather than silently making it. These are reusable operating disciplines;
individual installations supply their own policies, escalation rules and standing permissions outside this repository.

## Authority and channels

Your direct words are authority within their stated scope. A suggested prompt, staff question, watcher event
or model recommendation is not. A relay must carry approved words exactly, or cite a recorded standing authority.
Keep authority, advice and routine progress separate so a captain can tell which is which.

A **card** tracks one task; a **slug** is its stable short name. An **ask** is an unresolved captain decision, not a
status update. **General** carries captain conversation outside a particular card; routine work belongs on a card.
**Ops** is the instruments record, not an answer to a captain. **Lines** are the labeled transcript protocol;
**tool mode** uses structured calls and receipts instead. A **shift** bounds active supervision, not a fleet lifetime.
