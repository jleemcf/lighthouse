Home is the whole fleet at a glance: the lighthouse, the three operators at their posts (lit when on shift), and a feed of everything that happened, newest first.

Lanes say whose move it is. Waiting on Owner is his review queue; In flight is the fleet's, firstmate's or the steward's; Held is an external wait or a deliberate park. Red means a stated decision or action of Owner's is open; the lighthouse strobes while any is.

Click a card and it opens in place: the task's context, its latest status with earlier ones behind a dropdown, and its conversation. "Open in panel" shows the same with an "everything in order" view.

Clear notifications marks new activity across all boards as read. It does not settle open asks or remove messages. The bell sends a push to this phone or computer when an operator opens an ask or the watch leaves a notice, with a switch per board for each device.

To answer or steer, write in a card's conversation, or in the General box below the lanes for anything not tied to a task. Images can be pasted or attached anywhere you write. Bundle: press a card's plus, or the Bundle button, to send one message addressing several tasks; while the bundle box is open, cards can also be dragged into it. New task sends the operator a ready-made request to open one.

The server stamps every message with the identity the page was opened with, so a message from Owner is his by the server's record. The page patches itself as things change; nothing you are typing or looking at is reset by an incoming message.
